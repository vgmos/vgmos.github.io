In 2017, for EEE F366 with Dr. Pravin Mane at BITS Pilani, I studied gm/ID sizing in Cadence Virtuoso using the 45 nm GPDK. The project targeted a two-stage unbuffered op-amp. My September notes recorded a sized single-stage OTA and a planned extension to two stages.

The motivation was to ground transistor sizing in short-channel device behavior instead of relying on long-channel equations. The work plan combined hand calculations and Cadence simulation, with gain, gain-bandwidth product, and slew rate as design tradeoffs.
