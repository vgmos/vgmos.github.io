Steady-state operation

Across the ON-time tON, the inductor sees vIN − vOUT and its current ramps up; across the OFF-time tOFF, it sees −vOUT and its current ramps down. In CCM, volt-second balance sets the duty cycle: D = tON/tSW = vOUT/vIN.

The peak-to-peak inductor-current ripple is ΔiL = tON · (vIN − vOUT)/LX, or equivalently ΔiL = (vIN − vOUT) · D/(fSW · LX).

ΔiL/iO of roughly 25–50 % is a reasonable starting point.

When DCM is enabled, the tool first checks the CCM valley current against the selected zero-cross threshold. In DCM, duty also depends on load and the zero-current interval.

The power stage uses ideal switches, no dead time, and lossless inductors and capacitors. Optional output ESR adds iC · RESR to voltage ripple without changing duty cycle or inductor current. For power-loss estimates, use the Buck Converter Loss Tool.
